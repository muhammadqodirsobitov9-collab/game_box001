# Privacy Policy for GameBox

*Last updated: [DATE — fill in before publishing]*

This policy explains what data GameBox collects and how it's used.
**Short version: none of your data leaves your device.**

## What GameBox stores

GameBox stores the following information **locally on your device
only**, using Android/iOS's standard app-local storage
(`shared_preferences`). It is never transmitted anywhere:

- Your high scores, play counts, and progress (XP, level, coins,
  unlocked achievements) for each game
- Your favorited games
- Your app settings (dark/light theme, language, sound/vibration
  toggles)
- A display name, if you set one for the in-app leaderboard screen
- If you use the Admin Panel: a hashed (not plaintext) PIN you choose,
  and any game packages you import

## What GameBox does NOT do

- **No account or sign-up.** GameBox doesn't ask for your name, email,
  or any personal identifier.
- **No analytics or tracking.** GameBox doesn't use any third-party
  analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDK.
- **No ads.** GameBox shows no advertising of any kind.
- **No network requests for gameplay.** All 50 games run fully
  offline. The in-app "leaderboard" compares your real local score
  against a simulated demo field generated on your device — it is not
  sent to or compared against any server.
- **No data sharing.** Since nothing is collected, nothing is shared
  with third parties.

## Device permissions

- **File access** is requested only if you use the Admin Panel's
  "Import ZIP package" feature, and only to read the specific file you
  choose to import. GameBox does not scan or access other files on
  your device.

## Data deletion

Since all data stays on your device, uninstalling the app (or using
Settings → "Reset all data" inside the app) permanently deletes
everything GameBox has stored. There is no server-side copy to
separately request deletion of.

## Children's privacy

GameBox collects no personal information from anyone, including
children, consistent with COPPA and similar regulations.

## Changes to this policy

If GameBox ever adds features that change what data is collected
(for example, a real online leaderboard requiring a server), this
policy will be updated first, and this document should be revised to
accurately describe the new behavior before that feature ships.
