## Full description

GameBox is a pocket arcade of 50 original mini-games — arcade
reflexes, brain puzzles, board-game classics, and casual time-killers
— all in one app, all playable completely offline.

**50 games across 4 categories**
- Arcade: Snake, Tetris Lite, Alien Shooter, Flappy Block, Pong, Air
  Hockey, Brick Breaker, Target Shooter, Basketball Shootout, Rhythm
  Tap, Fruit Slice, Whack-a-Mole, Endless Runner, and more
- Puzzle: Sudoku, Minesweeper, 2048, Checkers, Sokoban, 15 Puzzle,
  Connect Four, Battleship, Bubble Pop, Peg Solitaire, Maze Escape
- Brain: Wordle, Word Scramble, Hangman, Anagram Hunter, Tower of
  Hanoi, Quiz Trivia, Speed Math, Simon Says, Memory Puzzle
- Casual: Blackjack, Darts, Tic-Tac-Toe, Higher or Lower, Rock Paper
  Scissors, Dice Duel, Coin Flip Streak, Balloon Inflate

**Level up as you play**
Every game feeds into one XP and coin system. Level up, unlock
achievements, and track your high score in each game from your
profile.

**Play your way**
- Fully offline — no internet connection needed to play
- No account or sign-up required
- Dark and light themes
- English, Russian, and Uzbek language support
- No ads, ever

**Grow the collection**
New game packages can be downloaded from within the app, and the
built-in Admin Panel lets you publish your own content packs (like
custom word lists) without needing an update.
